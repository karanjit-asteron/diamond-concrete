Flatness and levelness

Commercial floors measured beyond appearance.

Floor flatness and floor levelness can influence equipment movement, interior installations and the long-term use of a commercial space. We use proper concrete-finishing equipment to support the FF/FL objectives defined for the slab.

FF / FL

Floor flatness describes surface variation over shorter distances. Floor levelness relates to changes in elevation over longer distances.
