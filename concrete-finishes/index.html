Concrete finishes

The finish shapes how concrete looks and feels.

A concrete finish is more than the final visual layer. It defines the surface texture and helps the concrete relate to its location, intended use and surrounding design.

Diamond Concrete Works Ltd. offers three distinct finish options. The right selection depends on the project area, functional requirements and the appearance you want.

01 / TEXTURED

Broom Finish

A broom finish creates fine, visible lines across the concrete surface. Its straightforward texture gives exterior concrete a clean, practical appearance.

  • Linear surface texture
  • Commonly considered for sidewalks, driveways and ramps
  • Simple, functional visual character
02 / DECORATIVE

Exposed Aggregate

Exposed aggregate reveals the stone within the concrete, producing a naturally varied surface with visible texture, colour and detail.

  • Visible stone and aggregate texture
  • Suitable when the project calls for a more decorative surface
  • Natural variation across the completed concrete
03 / PATTERNED

Stamped Concrete

Stamped concrete uses an applied pattern to give the surface a more customized decorative appearance while retaining concrete as the base material.

  • Patterned surface appearance
  • Considered for decorative walkways and exterior areas
  • Strong visual definition within the project design
