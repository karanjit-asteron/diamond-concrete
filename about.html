About Diamond

Concrete work shaped by precision and purpose.

Diamond Concrete Works Ltd. focuses on commercial slabs, exterior site work and residential concrete. Our scope includes suspended slabs, slab on grade, structural slabs, floors, sidewalks, driveways, ramps, steps and curbs.

Every surface has a job to do. We approach each project with the equipment, preparation and finishing process needed for its intended use, desired appearance and project requirements.

Commercial capability

Equipment selected for demanding slabs.

For commercial floors, flatness and levelness can affect equipment operation, installation work and the long-term use of a building. Our concrete-finishing equipment supports the FF/FL objectives established for the project.

  • Commercial slab placement and finishing equipment
  • Support for project-specific floor flatness and levelness goals
  • Careful coordination from preparation through final finish
