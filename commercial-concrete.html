Commercial concrete

The slab is part of how the building works.

Commercial concrete floors can support equipment movement, interior systems, storage, public circulation and day-to-day operations. Their placement and finish must respond to those demands.

Diamond Concrete Works Ltd. approaches each commercial slab around its intended use, structural requirements, access conditions, planned finish and project-specific floor tolerances.

FF/FL objectives

Flat and level by more than appearance.

Commercial slabs may have specified floor flatness and floor levelness requirements. We use appropriate placement and finishing equipment to support the FF/FL objectives established for the project.

FFFloor flatness relates to shorter-distance surface variation.
FLFloor levelness relates to elevation variation over longer distances.
