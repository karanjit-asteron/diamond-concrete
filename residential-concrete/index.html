Finish options

Choose the surface character.

The appropriate finish depends on where the concrete is placed, how it will be used and the appearance you want to achieve.

Broom Finish

A practical textured surface commonly selected for exterior concrete where traction is important.

Exposed Aggregate

A decorative surface that reveals the concrete aggregate for added texture and visual detail.

Stamped Concrete

A patterned decorative finish that gives patios, walkways and other surfaces a distinct appearance.
